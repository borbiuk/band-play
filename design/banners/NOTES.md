# Store banners

Final files (PNG, 24-bit RGB, no alpha, as the Chrome Web Store requires):
- `marquee-1400x560.png`
- `small-promo-440x280.png`
Retina sources: `out/*@2x.png`. Editable sources: `banner-marquee.html`, `banner-small.html`, `banners.css` (open in a browser; export both PNGs with `node design/banners/export-banners.mjs` from the repo root).

## Copy
- Headline: "Play more. Click less."
- Subline (marquee): "Autoplay, shortcuts, speed and pitch control, and one-click batch downloads."
- Small promo: no UI screenshot. Big lockup (our logo, "for", the Bandcamp aqua badge), then "Band Play", "Play more. Click less." and "Autoplay, shortcuts and batch downloads."
- Chips (marquee): Autoplay, Shortcuts, Speed & pitch, Batch download.
- Legal line (marquee): "Unofficial extension. Not affiliated with Bandcamp."

Other headline options: "Bandcamp, on your terms." / "Your Bandcamp, on autopilot." / "Listen longer. Download faster." / "Play it your way."

## Bandcamp logo
Official aqua button "bc circle" from bandcamp.com/buttons (`assets/bandcamp-bc-circle-aqua.png`, color #1DA0C3), used after the word "for" in the lockup. Taken unchanged: no recoloring, cropping or effects. The page gives no usage terms beyond "Grab a button", so the risk that the store treats the banner as an imitation of Bandcamp is the same one we accepted for the icon.

## Visuals
The marquee shows the real prototypes (batch download bar on a collection, and the popup) scaled down. They are the redesigned UI, not the current one: use these banners only together with the new release.
