🎵 Bandcamp Play — Enhanced playback and batch download for Bandcamp

Power up Bandcamp with keyboard shortcuts, media keys, speed and pitch control, autoplay, a feed player, marks for what you already heard, and batch download of your purchases. Lightweight, private, and open source.

✨ Key features

- ⌨️ Keyboard shortcuts: Play/Pause, next and previous track, seek, speed, pitch, loop, open in a new tab and wishlist. Every shortcut can be changed in the popup
- 🎧 Media keys: The play, pause, next, previous and seek keys of your keyboard or headset control the Bandcamp player
- 🕒 Speed and pitch: Change the speed in 1% steps, with or without pitch preservation
- ⏩ Seek step: Seek forward and back by 1 to 120 seconds (10 by default); digit keys jump to 0%, 10%, … 90% of the track
- ▶️ Autoplay and autoscroll: The next track of the album, collection, wishlist, feed or discover list starts by itself, and the page scrolls to it
- 🔁 Loop track: Repeat the current track
- 🎨 Highlight visited: Albums and tracks you already opened or played are marked on Bandcamp pages
- 🧩 Feed player: A small player with cover, title and progress bar on the feed page
- 📥 Batch download: Select items on your collection or purchases page (one by one, a range with Shift+click, all, or only the new ones) and download them in MP3 V0, MP3 320, FLAC, AAC, Ogg Vorbis, ALAC, WAV or AIFF
- 📋 Downloads page: See the overall progress, speed and time left; filter, search and sort the list; pause, resume, retry, cancel or remove items; set how many files download at the same time (1 to 15)
- ⚙️ Popup: Four tabs (Playback, Page, Downloads, Shortcuts); turn each shortcut on or off, record a new key, skip releases you already downloaded, get a notification when a batch finishes, import, export or reset your settings
- 💤 Keep Awake (Chrome): Keeps the display on while you listen

🎹 Default shortcuts

- ␣ Space — Play/Pause
- N / B — Next / Previous track
- Shift + 1…9 — Play that track of the list
- Shift + V — Loop track on or off
- ⬅️ ➡️ Arrow Left/Right — Seek backward/forward
- 0–9 — Jump to 0%, 10%, … 90% of the track
- ⬆️ ⬇️ Arrow Up/Down — Speed up/down by 1%
- Shift + ⬇️ — Reset speed
- P — Pitch preservation on or off; Shift + P — Turn it off
- O — Open the current item in a new tab; Shift + O — Open it and switch to the tab
- L — Add to or remove from the wishlist
- 🔗 Full list: https://github.com/borbiuk/band-play#keyboard-shortcuts

🔒 Privacy

- No data collection. No analytics. The extension sends nothing anywhere and has no servers of its own.
- The only network traffic it causes is for batch download: it loads the Bandcamp download pages of the items you selected and downloads the files from Bandcamp's own download links.
- Your settings, the visited history and the download history are stored locally in the browser (extension local storage) and never leave your computer.
- Open source: https://github.com/borbiuk/band-play

🧾 Permissions (what and why)

- 🗂️ Tabs — Opens the current item in a new tab, opens the downloads page, and tells the page when you move to another Bandcamp page.
- 💾 Storage — Saves your settings, shortcuts, the visited history and the download queue and history locally.
- ⬇️ Downloads — Saves the files of a batch download and shows, pauses, resumes, cancels or deletes them from the downloads page.
- ⏰ Alarms — Keeps a long batch download going and retries it later when Bandcamp asks to slow down.
- 🔋 Power (Chrome only) — Keeps the display awake while Keep Awake is on.
- 🧺 Downloads shelf and Downloads UI (Chrome only) — Hides the browser's download bubble while a batch download runs, so it does not pop up for every file, and shows it again afterwards.
- 🔔 Notifications (optional) — Asked only when you turn on "notify when a batch finishes". Shows one notification when a batch download ends.
- 🌐 Access to bandcamp.com and its subdomains — Runs the player features on Bandcamp pages and loads the Bandcamp download pages for batch download. The extension runs on no other site.

🦊 Firefox

- Works in Firefox 128 or newer. Keep Awake is not available in Firefox, so it is hidden there.
